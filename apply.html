How to apply

Four steps, in order.

  1. Register your interest

    Sign up on millenniumfellows.org so you're notified when the Class of 2027 application opens.

  2. Pick one SDG and one problem you can touch

    Start local. Something on campus, in Pudong, or in a community you already belong to.

  3. Draft a project you can finish in a semester

    Name who it helps, what you'll do, and how you'll measure it. Tie it to a UN Academic Impact principle.

  4. Talk to your Point of Contact

    Bring your draft to Kat for feedback and advice on references. Earlier is always better.

What you're signing up for

The honest version

  • One semester, August to November
  • At least six on-campus sessions with your cohort
  • A project you actually run, not just propose
  • A report and a story of impact at the end

Questions

Asked every year.

Who can apply?

University undergraduates leading, or ready to lead, a project on sustainable development. You apply as an individual, but campuses host cohorts of 8 to 20+, so bring your friends: a strong NYU Shanghai applicant pool helps everyone.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The program is free for accepted applicants.

How much time does it take?

It runs for one semester, roughly August to November. Expect at least six on-campus sessions, optional weekly global webinars, and the real work: running your project.

What counts as a project?

Anything time-bound that makes meaningful, measurable impact during the fellowship and advances at least one SDG and one UN Academic Impact principle. A campus campaign, a community partnership, a tool, a program: if you can measure it by December, it counts.

Can I lead the cohort?

Yes. If you advance to Round 2, the same application lets you apply to be one of two Campus Directors. They run the campus sessions and keep the cohort moving.

What do I get?

A certificate from United Nations Academic Impact and MCN when you graduate, a public fellow profile, a place in the global alumni community, and a semester of real leadership experience you can talk about in any interview.

When can I apply?

Applications for the Class of 2026 are closed. Register your interest for the Class of 2027 on the official site so you hear the moment it opens, then email Kat.
