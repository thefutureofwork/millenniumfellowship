SDG Workshop

Co-planned with our Campus Directors and built around your projects, not a generic slideshow. First, a five-minute needs survey:

  • Which SDGs you know, and which your project serves
  • What stage your project is at
  • The skills you need most right now
  • Your biggest roadblock

Date and format come from your Campus Directors. Check your email.

The rule for projects

Every project must be…

  • Time-bound: meaningful, measurable impact within the fellowship semester
  • Tied to at least one SDG
  • Tied to at least one UN Academic Impact principle

Stuck on scope? Email Kat. A smaller project you finish beats a big one you don't.
