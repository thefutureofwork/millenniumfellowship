On our campus

From one nomination to the whole program.

  1. 2023

    The Fellowship launches at NYU Shanghai. Katherine Tosi (kt1992@nyu.edu) begins nominating students, serving as a referee and advisor, and presenting to fellows on approaches and resources.

  2. 2023 to 2025

    Builds the pipeline from the classroom: first-year courses on the SDGs and sustainability send students toward the Fellowship.

  3. 2026

    Appointed Campus Point of Contact and joins the MCN Civic Learning Council as an Associate Member.

What the role means

The campus's main line to the Fellowship

  • Main contact for Fellowship outreach and questions
  • Sharing opportunities with eligible students
  • Supporting fellows and Campus Directors through the semester
  • Taking part in MCN Civic Learning Council convenings and best-practice exchange
